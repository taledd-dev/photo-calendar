Style reference — the site as it is today
This page recreates the current look of photoplan.studio without the app: the colour tokens, the type system, and the real components, serialised from the running site with their own markup and inline styles. Use the switch in the corner for night mode. Whole-page snapshots are in pages/: home · month · month-visitor · trips · gallery · gallery-review · weather · map · month-phone.
Generated 2026-11-17 from index.html by design/build-reference.js — sample data only.
Principles
  • A printed field-notebook feel: warm cream paper on a dotted, slightly darker page; near-black warm ink; olive green for actions and the UK, terracotta for International and warnings.
  • Three type voices: Zilla Slab for names and titles, Spectral for prose, IBM Plex Mono in small tracked capitals for every label, button and datum. Figtree only for the wordmark.
  • Square corners (radius 0–4px), 1px hairlines, and hard offset shadows (e.g. 3px 3px 0) rather than soft blurs; hover nudges an element up-left by 1px.
  • Content column 1180px max, 26px page gutters (13px on phones, breakpoint 640px); cards pad 16–22px; rows in cards are a 74px label column plus content, separated by soft rules.
  • Colour is semantic and tokenised: surfaces, lines, inks, then accent families (green, terracotta, gold/amber, slate) each with a wash, an ink and an accent. Night mode swaps token values only.
  • Photographs are never recoloured; controls drawn over a photo use the fixed “on-photo” tokens in both themes.

Golden hour & sunsets
Promising at Mam Tor: Thu 19 Nov (sunset)
Conditions
Broken mid and high cloud (roughly 30–70 %) with a clear strip of horizon in the sun’s direction — best just after a front has cleared. Overcast kills it; a cloudless sky gives clean light but little colour.
Settings
f/8–f/11, ISO 100, tripod. Bracket ±2 EV or use a graduated ND for the bright sky. Expose for the sky and lift shadows later.
Tips
Be in place 30 min before sunset and stay 20 min after — the afterglow often beats the sun itself. Check cloud at the sunset hour in the outlook above, not the daily summary.
